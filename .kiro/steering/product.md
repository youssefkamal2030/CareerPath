# Product Overview

CareerPath is a comprehensive job search and career management platform that leverages AI to help users find the best career opportunities. The platform provides:

## Core Features

- **AI-Powered CV Analysis**: Automated extraction and analysis of CV/resume data
- **Job Recommendations**: Intelligent job matching based on user profiles and skills
- **Application Tracking**: Complete job application lifecycle management
- **Company Profiles**: Detailed company information and job listings
- **User Authentication**: Secure registration, login, and password management

## Business Domain

The platform operates in the career services and recruitment industry, connecting job seekers with opportunities through:

- Personal profile management with skills, experience, and education tracking
- Company and job posting management
- Application status tracking and follow-up reminders
- AI-driven career recommendations and matching algorithms

## Target Users

- Job seekers looking for career opportunities
- Companies posting job openings
- Career advisors and recruitment professionals

## Deployment
